Why UNWRITTEN exists

A companion to your notebook, never a replacement.

Notebooks and journals matter. The pen, the paper, the slower pace of handwriting: UNWRITTEN does not try to take any of that away.

But some days the notebook is not with you. You are on a bus, in a queue, away from home, or a thought turns up at midnight and your bag is across the room. UNWRITTEN is a place for that thought to land, so it is still there when you get back to your pages.

Use it with your notebook

  1. Catch it here when paper is out of reach.
  2. Save it as a PNG, or print it as a PDF.
  3. Rewrite it by hand in your journal when you can. That keeps the habit going.

Your writing stays on this device. If you sign in, it is saved to your own private account and shown to no one else.

Scroll less, write a little

  1. Scrolling fills your head with other people’s thoughts. Writing gives you yours back.
  2. Putting a feeling into words tends to make it feel smaller. Researchers call this affect labelling.
  3. Studies link long stretches of doomscrolling with more stress, and expressive writing with a slightly better mood.

Just research, not medical advice. A few lines a day is plenty.

Privacy, in plain words

  • If you do not sign in, everything you write stays in this browser and goes nowhere.
  • If you sign in, we keep your email and four things: your posts, notes, letter and writing days. Nothing else. No ads and no trackers.
  • They are stored with Google Firebase. The rules let only your own account read your writing. The person who runs UNWRITTEN can technically open the database for maintenance, but does not read or share your writing.
  • You are in control. Sign out removes it all from this device. Erase everything also deletes your saved copy. Delete account in your profile removes your account and its data.
